# 双屏休闲全景背景

采用 imagegen 内置工具生成单张连续全景，再机械缩放至 1280×480 并按 x=640 切分。未修改 HDL、bit 或 TF 卡。

- A 左屏：relax_A_left_640x480.bmp
- B 右屏：relax_B_right_640x480.bmp
- 两张 BMP 均为 640×480、24 位、BI_RGB 无压缩、正高度。
- 背景没有星点、时间或 UI，供 FPGA 实时叠加。
- 两屏需左 A 右 B 排列；本次只提供素材，现有固件是否能分别加载两张背景需后续接入。

## 最终提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: single continuous panoramic background for two side-by-side car relaxation displays. Create ONE ultra-wide 8:3 landscape panorama, composed for 1280x480, to split at the center into two distinct 640x480 screens. Photorealistic rolling grassy hills at blue-hour twilight, dark emerald grass with visible fine natural blades, gently undulating foreground ridge continuous across the entire image, distant low hills. Left half a shallow layered valley, right half a rounded grassy rise; seamlessly continuous terrain at center, one camera viewpoint, consistent scale and lighting. Upper roughly 60 percent quiet clean smooth indigo navy sky fading into muted lavender near horizon, similar to a refined tranquil nighttime grassland wallpaper. Beautiful understated cinematic atmosphere, adequately visible grass detail, not crushed black. Full bleed landscape only. NO stars, NO fireflies, NO glowing points, NO moon, NO sun, NO text or time, NO UI, NO vehicles, NO screen bezel, NO divider, NO collage, NO watermark. This is one continuous photograph, not two separate panels.
